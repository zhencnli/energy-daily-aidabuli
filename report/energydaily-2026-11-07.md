### 今日英语单词：referendum
- 英式音标：/ˌrefəˈrendəm/；美式音标：/ˌrefəˈrendəm/
- 词性：名词（noun）
- 释义：① 全民公投，指就某一特定政策或政治问题由全体选民直接投票决定；② 公民投票程序。英文释义：a vote in which all the people of a country or an area are asked to give their opinion about or decide an important political or social question.
- 例句1：Voters in several states used local referendums to restrict new data center construction.（多个州选民通过地方公投限制新建数据中心。）
- 例句2：The referendum result could delay the project's permitting timeline.（公投结果可能拖慢项目的许可审批时间。）
