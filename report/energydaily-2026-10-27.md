## 今日英语单词
**electrification**（英 /ɪˌlektrɪfɪˈkeɪʃn/；美 /ɪˌlektrɪfɪˈkeɪʃn/）
- 词性：名词（noun）
- 释义：电气化；指将交通、供热、工业等终端用能由化石燃料转为电力驱动的过程。英文释义：the process of changing a system or area so that it uses electricity instead of other forms of energy.
- 例句1：Rapid electrification of transport and heating is increasing electricity demand.（交通和供热快速电气化正在推高电力需求。）
- 例句2：Grid planners need to account for the electrification of industry and buildings.（电网规划者需要把工业和建筑电气化纳入考量。）
