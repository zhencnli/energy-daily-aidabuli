## 今日英语单词

**uprate**
- 音标：英 /ˌʌpˈreɪt/；美 /ˌʌpˈreɪt/
- 词性：动词（亦可作名词 uprate）
- 释义：英 to increase the maximum level of power, output, or value that something can produce；中 提高（设备、机组等的）额定功率、产能或价值；升级扩容。
- 例句1：Constellation plans to **uprate** 11 nuclear reactors across PJM, adding 890 MW of capacity by 2032.
- 例句2：A nuclear **uprate** can deliver firm power faster than building a brand-new plant.
